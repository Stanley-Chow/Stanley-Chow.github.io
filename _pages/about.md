I am an undergraduate student in Mathematics and Computer Science at The University of Hong Kong.

My academic interests lie in numerical analysis, scientific machine learning, data-driven optimization, and quantitative research. I am particularly interested in using mathematical modeling and computational methods to study inverse problems, optimization problems, and data-driven decision-making.

I am currently an HKU Summer Research Fellow, working on neural-augmented reduced-order methods for PDE inverse problems. My current research focuses on proper orthogonal decomposition, a posteriori regularization parameter selection, and robustness evaluation for parabolic inverse source problems.

Beyond research, I have worked on quantitative factor modeling, retrieval-augmented generation systems, flight price prediction, seismic data processing, and urban transportation network optimization.

This website collects my research, projects, notes, and academic updates.
