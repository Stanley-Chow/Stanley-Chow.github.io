# Featured Projects

## WorldQuant International Quant Championship (IQC)

**Quantitative Research** | **Python** | **Factor Investing**

Conducted quantitative factor research using the WorldQuant Brain platform. Developed and backtested over 40 alpha factors across momentum, value, volatility, profitability, and sentiment dimensions. Built multi-factor long-short portfolios and evaluated performance using Sharpe ratio, turnover, and drawdown metrics.

**Keywords:** Quantitative Finance, Factor Modeling, Portfolio Construction, Backtesting

## Flight Price Prediction

**Machine Learning** | **XGBoost** | **Regression**

Developed machine learning models to predict airline ticket prices using flight metadata and temporal information. Compared polynomial regression with XGBoost and investigated the "last-minute effect" through feature importance analysis and partial dependence plots.

**Keywords:** Machine Learning, XGBoost, Regression, Explainable AI
